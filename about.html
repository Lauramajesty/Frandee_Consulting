About Us

Driving Trust and Precision

Our Expertise

FRANDEE Consulting Services is a team of dedicated geoscientists and engineers with strong expertise in groundwater assessment, mineral exploration, and subsurface characterization.

We understand the geological complexity of the Niger Delta. Our experience allows us to provide practical, science-driven solutions for water resource development, construction planning, and environmental assessments.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality geoscience services through rigorous data collection, dependable analysis, and clear reporting that empowers clients to make informed decisions.

Our Vision

To be a trusted leader in Petrophysics, groundwater, and mineral exploration across West Africa, delivering solutions that strengthen sustainable development.

Our Approach

We integrate field data, laboratory results, and modern geophysical interpretation tools to ensure every investigation is thorough and technically sound.

Quality & HSE Commitment

Safety and professional integrity guide our operations. We follow best practices from fieldwork to final reporting to ensure dependable results and safe delivery.
